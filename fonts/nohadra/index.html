Typeface family · v1.006

The Nohadra Syriac fonts are a bold, geometric and (mostly) monospaced Syriac typeface family, with square, block-like characters and uniform line thickness. Every outline is drawn on a grid of 101-unit squares. Sapna is the minimal block style; Amedia has the same look and feel with rounder edges.
